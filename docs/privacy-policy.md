# Privacy Policy for Glint Reader

Effective: 2026-11-12

Published at https://navica.llc/tools/glint-reader/privacy/. Keep this file and that page in step.

Glint Reader is a Chrome extension published by Navica LLC. It reads web pages aloud with
word highlighting. This policy explains what it handles. The short version: it collects
nothing, and it sends nothing to us.

## What the extension reads

- **Page text, only when you start it.** The extension can't see a page until you press the
  shortcut, click its toolbar icon, or choose Read aloud from the right-click menu on that tab. It then reads the visible text of that page so
  it can speak it and highlight each word. The text is used on the spot and is never stored or
  sent to us.

## What the extension stores

These are kept in Chrome's own extension storage on your computer:

- **Your settings:** voice, reading speed, and highlight colors. These use Chrome's sync storage,
  so if you have turned on Chrome sync, Google syncs them between your own browsers.
- **Measured top speeds:** the name of each voice you measured and how fast it can go. This stays
  on this computer only.

Removing the extension deletes both.

## Voices

The extension speaks through Chrome's text to speech feature. On-device voices, such as the
voices built into macOS and Windows, speak entirely on your computer. Some voices are online
voices, marked "Online" in the settings. When you choose one, Chrome sends the text being spoken
to that voice's provider, such as Google, to turn it into speech. That provider's privacy policy
covers that text. Choose an on-device voice if you want nothing to leave your computer.

## What we never do

- We don't collect, sell, or share any data. The extension has no servers, no account, and no
  analytics.
- The extension doesn't read pages you haven't started it on, and it doesn't keep any record of
  the pages you read.
- It loads no code from outside the extension.
