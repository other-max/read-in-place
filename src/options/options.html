Glint Reader

Press Alt+Shift+R (Option+Shift+R on a Mac) on any page to start or stop reading.

Voice

Speed

Each step is the speed you hear, and the fastest step is the top speed your voice can really reach. On-device voices usually go well past 2x. Online voices can sound rough near their top speed.

Highlight colors

New colors apply the next time you start reading on a page.

Keys while reading

Alt+Shift+RStart or stop on the current tab
Click textRead from that word (Alt+click on links)
SpacePause or resume
→ / ←Next or previous sentence
↓ / ↑Next or previous paragraph
= / -Faster or slower (] / [ also work)
0Back to 1x
EscStop
Corner controlsHover the pill in the bottom-right corner to pause, change speed, open settings, or stop
